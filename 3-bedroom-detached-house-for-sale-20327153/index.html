ABOUT YOUR HOME

Step into the Hazel and discover a welcoming entrance hallway, complete with a WC and handy storage space. At the heart of the home is a stylish open-plan kitchen and dining area, with French doors that open out to the garden - perfect for relaxing or entertaining. The layout flows seamlessly into a bright and spacious front-aspect living room, bathed in natural light. Upstairs, you'll find two generously sized double bedrooms, including an en-suite to the main bedroom, along with a versatile single bedroom and a sleek, modern family bathroom. This home also benefits from a single garage and two parking spaces.

ABOUT YOUR DEVELOPMENT

Wootton Grange is the latest superb development of 450 high-quality new build homes in Norfolk from Allison Homes with a mix of stunning three, four and five-bedroom homes, all finished to a high standard.

Wootton Grange is located within the small, picturesque Norfolk village of South Wootton, King's Lynn, set amidst un-spoilt natural beauty. The village is expertly positioned in the idyllic Norfolk countryside, with the bustling market town of King's Lynn to the south-west, a stretch of Norfolk's famous coastline to the north, the River Great Ouse to the west, and the famous 12th Century Castle Rising to the north-east. Known for its stunning and peaceful outdoor rural spaces, there remains a variety of woodlands and spectacular historic scenery to explore.

South Wootton has its own infant school, and junior school (both OFSTED GOOD), post office, doctor's surgery, alongside a church, a variety of village shops, social clubs, and pubs.

The villages of Wolferton and Sandringham are both approximately seven miles away from South Wootton, and boost beautiful walks through nature and the English countryside at its finest.

It is also well connected to the major cities of Norwich (via the A47) and Cambridge (via a regular train service) for a day out shopping in the city. Less than three miles away from South Wootton is King's Lynn Railway Station providing regular trains to London Kings Cross and access to the entire national rail network.
